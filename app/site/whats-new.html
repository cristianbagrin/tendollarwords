What’s new

Version 5.0, November 2026.

  • Nothing else needed

    Your drafts live in this browser, and in your account when you sign in. Coming from Notion? Settings → Account → Import brings your pages over.

  • Keep a whole book together

    A draft can hold its chapters, in order. Read, copy or export the lot as one manuscript.

  • Catch an idea before it goes

    Jot it down from anywhere, then turn it into a draft or a chapter when you’re ready.

  • Small mistakes fix themselves

    Typos, doubled words and missing capitals are cleaned up as you go. ⌘Z takes any of them back.

  • Made for your phone too

    Swipe from the left for your drafts. Everything you tap sits within reach of your thumb.

  • Settings in one place

    One window, with a short note wherever you need one.

  • A new address

    The app now lives at /dashboard.
