What’s new

Version 5.0, November 2026.

  • Write without anything else

    Ten Dollar Words stands on its own now. Your drafts live in your account, and Notion isn’t needed.

  • Keep a whole book together

    Projects hold their parts, in order. Read, copy or export the lot as one manuscript.

  • Catch an idea before it goes

    Jot it down from anywhere, then turn it into a draft when you’re ready.

  • Small mistakes fix themselves

    Typos, doubled words and missing capitals are cleaned up as you go. ⌘Z takes any of them back.

  • Make it yours

    New settings, all in one window, with a short note wherever you need one.

  • A new address

    The app now lives at /dashboard. Come back here any time to see what changed.
