How it works

Write first. Cut after.

  1. Write

    Open a draft and type. Nothing gets in your way, and nothing moves.

  2. Review

    Press ⌘E, or tap Review on your phone. Wordy phrases get underlined, and each note says what cutting it saves.

  3. Cut

    Take the fix, or keep your words and ignore the note. Your text only changes when you say so.

Try it on something you wrote.
