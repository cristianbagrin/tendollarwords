Privacy

Your writing is yours.

Your drafts stay with you

Without an account, everything you write stays in this browser.

Synced, and locked

Sign in with your cristianbagrin.com account and your drafts and ideas follow you to every device. They’re encrypted on your device before they’re sent, so not even I can read them.

AI is optional

Turn it on with your own Google Gemini key. Only the words you ask about are sent to Google, and only when you ask.

Notion, once

Import from Notion uses your Notion secret to copy your pages here, then forgets it.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. Nothing is sold.
